Project description

Conducted project is a part of the Data Visualization course at the University of Genoa.

The main goal of it is to create an interactive website that will present data on pollution in a clear and engaging way.

Task 1

First visualization assignmest was divided into three parts:
  • Presenting charts for Top 10 most polluting countries in 2022 and at least for the last decade
  • Presenting charts for Top 5 most polluting countries but taking into account the region of a country
  • Presenting CO₂ emissions from fossil fuels and land-use change in 2022

Task 2

Second assignment consisted of making alluvial plot. It is a type of flow diagram, in which the width of the bands is proportional to the flow quantity.
In our case we presented division of all continents into top 5 most polluting countries and then we also presented the division into two groups: fossil fuels and land-use change.


The project is still in progress and we are planning to add more visualizations and make the website more interactive.
