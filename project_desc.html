Project description

Conducted project is a part of the Data Visualization course at the University of Geneva. The main goal is to create an interactive website that will present data on CO₂ emissions per capita in a clear and engaging way.

First visualization assignmest was divided into three parts:

  • Presenting charts for Top 10 most polluting countries in 2022 and at least for the last decade
  • Presenting charts for Top 5 most polluting countries but taking into account the region of a country
  • Presenting CO₂ emissions from fossil fuels and land-use change in 2022 on a heatmap
