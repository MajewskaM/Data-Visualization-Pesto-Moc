Explanation

The chart was created based on data from the file co-emission-per-capita.csv.

The original dataset included a mixture of data types:

  • countries
  • continents
  • economic categories (such as "high income" and "low income")
To focus solely on individual countries for this analysis, we filtered the dataset to exclude rows that didn't correspond to specific nations. Using Python, we cleaned and processed the data to extract only the relevant rows for countries. We then ranked these countries based on their CO₂ emissions per capita and selected the top 10 emitters for 2022.

Finally, the cleaned and sorted data was imported into D3.js, where it was visualized as a bar chart to allow easy comparison of emissions across these top-polluting countries.

Comment

This interactive bar chart displays the Top 10 Most Polluting Countries based on CO₂ emissions per capita. The data is visualized for different time periods, such as the Last 5 Years (2018-2022), Last Decade (2013-2022), and Last 2 Decades (2003-2022).

Users can select the time range using a dropdown, and the chart dynamically updates to show the most polluting countries for the chosen period.

Key Insights:

  • In the last 5 years (2018-2022), Qatar had the highest CO₂ emissions per capita, averaging 18.51 tonnes per person.
  • In the last decade (2013-2022), Qatar again topped the chart with 37.53 tonnes per person.
  • Over the last two decades (2003-2022), Qatar maintained its position as the top emitter with 88.13 tonnes per person.
The data is presented in tonnes per capita, which reflects the average CO₂ emissions per person in each country for the respective time period.

Comments/Results

Asia has consistently led in CO₂ emissions per capita, with largest emitters:

  • 2000: Qatar - 62,41 CO2 per capita
  • 2010: Qatar - 42,85 CO2 per capita
  • 2015: Qatar - 37,77 CO2 per capita
  • 2020: Qatar - 37,13 CO2 per capita
  • 2022: Qatar - 37,60 CO2 per capita
Overall trend: decrease in CO₂ emissions per capita over the years in each continent The data shows that regions like Asia and Africa, are experiencing higher absolute emissions due to their larger populations. Additionally countries with the greatest population like Russia or USA have been placed high among TOP emiting countries.

Explanation

Chart represent TOP 5 countires with largest annual CO2 emissions per person, groupped by continents. Colors indicate the population of each country. Emissions for "Others" category is calculated using weighted average of each country, from given continent not included as TOP 5.
